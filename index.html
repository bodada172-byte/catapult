Catapult: Smash the City

One shot. How much can you destroy?

Pull back, aim and launch. Every building in Catapult is built from real pieces — hit a support column and watch the floors above come down; topple one tower into the next and start a chain collapse.

  • 17 cities to unlock, each wider and tougher than the last — with their own landmarks
  • 60 missiles in five families: heavy rams, penetrators, cluster bombs, wide-blast warheads and more
  • Upgrade your launch power and blast size to reach further and break more
  • Shot ratings — GOOD HIT, GREAT, AMAZING and the rare EXCELLENT — better shots pay bonus coins
  • Side goals in every city, reinforced buildings to crack, and gems for the best shots
  • Plays offline. No account needed. No real-money purchases.
