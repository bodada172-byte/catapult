Privacy Policy — Boomtropolis

Effective date: 2 November 2026 (updated for in-app purchases of gems)
Publisher: Catapult Studio
Contact: alasfoor.salem@gmail.com

Summary

Boomtropolis itself does not collect or store any personal information on a server. The game offers optional rewarded video ads, served by Google AdMob; when an ad is requested, Google collects some device information as described below. Watching an ad is always your choice. Gems can also be bought with real money through Google Play; Google handles the payment and we never see your payment details.

What the game stores

The game saves your progress (cities unlocked, missiles and upgrades, coins and gems, and settings such as sound and vibration) in a file on your own device. This file never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the game.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows rewarded video ads only when you choose to watch one (for example "WATCH AD +1 LEVEL" or to multiply the coins of a shot). The ads are provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares with Google: your device's advertising ID, IP address (which may be used to estimate your general location), device and app information, and how you interact with the ads. This data is sent encrypted. We do not receive it.

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time with PRIVACY CHOICES in the game's settings.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device's settings (Settings › Google › Ads, or Settings › Privacy › Ads).
  • How Google uses this data: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

In-app purchases (Google Play)

The GEM SHOP sells packs of gems, the game's virtual currency, for real money. Purchases are made and paid through Google Play Billing: Google processes the payment under the Google Payments Privacy Notice, and we never receive your card, bank or account details. The game receives only what is needed to deliver the gems (the product bought and Google's order number), checks it on your device and keeps the order number in the save file on your device so the same order is never delivered twice. Nothing about your purchases is sent to us or to any other service. Gems have no cash value and cannot be exchanged for money. Refunds are handled by Google Play.

What the game does not do

  • It does not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos or any other personal information.
  • It does not use analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • It does not sell anything except gem packs through Google Play, and it never handles payment details.
  • It does not require an account. Without an internet connection the game plays normally; only the optional ads are unavailable.

Permissions

The game asks for permission to vibrate the device (vibration feedback, which you can switch off in the game's settings), and the ads SDK adds internet access, network state and the advertising ID permission so that ads can be loaded.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from them.

Deleting your data

All game data is stored only on your device. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data in your device settings, deletes it completely. Data collected by Google for ads is handled under Google's policies; you can reset your advertising ID in your device's settings, or contact us and we will help with any request.

About this website

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no third-party scripts or fonts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may record visitors' IP addresses in its server logs for security, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Changes

If a future version changes how data is handled, this policy will be updated before that version is released. 31 October 2026: added rewarded ads by Google AdMob (version 0.9.1). 2 November 2026: added the gem shop, with purchases through Google Play.
