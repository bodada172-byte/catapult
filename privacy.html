Privacy Policy — Catapult: Smash the City

Effective date: 31 October 2026
Publisher: Catapult Studio
Contact: alasfoor.salem@gmail.com

Summary

Catapult does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information.

What the game stores

The game saves your progress (cities unlocked, missiles and upgrades, coins and gems, and settings such as sound and vibration) in a file on your own device. This file never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the game.

What the game does not do

  • It does not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information.
  • It does not use advertising identifiers, analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • It does not show ads and does not offer purchases with real money.
  • It does not require an account or an internet connection, and it does not request the internet permission.

Permissions

The game asks only for permission to vibrate the device, used for the vibration feedback you can switch off in the game's settings.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Deleting your data

All game data is stored only on your device. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data in your device settings, deletes it completely.

Changes

If a future version adds features that handle data (for example advertising or analytics), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be described here.

Contact

Questions about this policy: alasfoor.salem@gmail.com.
